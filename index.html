    HOW TO PLAY
    • ◀ ▶ move and ▲ jump. Hold a direction and tap ▲ to flip.
    • PUNCH and KICK to attack. Hold PUNCH for fast punches.
    • Land 2 or 3 kicks in a row, or 3 punches, for a big automatic combo.
    • BLOCK stops hits. Block right as a hit lands to PARRY.
    • Fill the blue bar, then tap FIRE for a fireball. A full bar fires a SUPER blast.
    • Win 2 rounds to beat a fighter. Then tap the moves shown on screen for a finishing move.
    • If your fighter falls, the next teammate jumps in. Low health turns on RAGE MODE.
    • Kaiser copies each opponent's style and can steal their finisher.
    • Beat a fighter to unlock them as a player.
    Keyboard: ← → move · ↑ jump · ↓ block · A punch · S kick · D fireball

# Apartment Maintenance Software Comparison

Nov 5, 2026

## Bottom line

Every major program does the same core job: a request comes in, a manager assigns it, a tech or vendor completes it on a phone, and the office sees the history. The big programs bury that job inside leasing, accounting and rent software, which is why their maintenance apps get complaints about speed, sync failures and too many clicks.

Our app does only maintenance, does it in fewer taps, and makes every button say exactly what it will do. Version 1 covers work orders, a tech phone app, vendors, a make-ready board, recurring tasks and spreadsheet import. Accounting, rent and leasing stay out.

## How each program handles maintenance

| Program | Type | How maintenance works | Weak spots users mention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Yardi Voyager](https://yardi.com/?p=3354) | All-in-one | Techs see assigned and unassigned work orders, add labor time, parts and notes, and close jobs on the [Maintenance Mobile app](https://apps.apple.com/app/id493578661). Works offline and syncs later. Recurring work orders and purchase orders built in. Vendor bids through an add-on called VendorCafe ([brochure](https://resources.yardi.com/documents/maintenance-brochure)). | The iOS app averages about 1.8 stars ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/493578661)). |
| [RealPage Facilities](https://www.realpage.com/onesite/facilities/) | All-in-one | Managers assign work orders to techs in the app. Techs record labor time, add comments by voice and close requests in the field. Inspections can charge residents for damage on the spot ([flyer](https://www.realpage.com/storage/files/blog/posts/pdfs/2020/5/c19-ppm-18-156-keep-your-maintenance-process-moving.pdf)). | Tied to RealPage; parts ordering is a separate add-on. |
| [Entrata Facilities](https://www.entrata.com:443/products/facility-management) | All-in-one | Techs view, edit and complete work orders and inspections on mobile. Digital make-ready boards replace whiteboards. | Reviewers ask for better speed, search and offline use ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/entrata/reviews_and_filters)); crashes and sync failures are a top complaint ([app analysis](https://marlvel.ai/api/llm/apps/business/entrata-facilities)). |
| [AppFolio](https://www.appfolio.com/features/maintenance) | All-in-one | A contact center auto-prioritizes emergencies and dispatches by rules. Drag-and-drop calendar for scheduling techs. Unit turn board. Vendors use a website portal, not an app, to accept jobs, upload invoices and mark work complete ([vendor portal](https://www.appfolio.com/help/vendor-portal)). | 50-unit minimum; built for management companies, not small properties. |
| [Property Meld](https://softwarefinder.com/property-management-software/property-meld) | Maintenance only | Coordinates residents, techs, vendors and owners in one place. Preventive maintenance scheduling and turnover projects. Syncs with AppFolio. | One reviewer called it expensive if your main software already handles maintenance ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.ie/software/149045/property-meld)). |
| [AppWork](https://www.softwareadvice.co.uk/software/441218/appwork) | Maintenance only | Work orders, digital make-ready board, inspections, tech performance tracking and a bilingual tech app ([AppFolio partner page](https://www.appfolio.com/partners/appwork)). Auto-assign can send all work orders for a unit to one tech ([release notes](https://appworkco.com/release-notes/smarter-inspections-auto-assign-and-more-february-25th-2025/)). | Add-on cost on top of the main software. |

The two maintenance-only programs, Property Meld and AppWork, are the closest model for us: they exist because the big programs' maintenance tools frustrate people.

## Their way vs. our way

| Feature | How the big programs do it | How we'd do it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requests coming in | Resident portal, office entry, call center | Office or manager enters it in under 30 seconds; resident portal added later |
| Assigning | Manager picks a tech; AppFolio and AppWork can auto-assign by rules | Manager picks a tech or vendor with one tap; optional "same unit, same tech" rule |
| Tech phone app | Separate app per program, often slow, needs syncing | One web app that installs to the home screen; big buttons, photo upload from camera, works with weak signal |
| Completing a job | Close the work order, add labor and parts | One "Complete" button with optional notes, photos, time and parts |
| Canceling a job | Cancel may or may not email the vendor depending on settings | Two clear buttons: "Cancel" and "Cancel and notify vendor"; the screen always says who gets told |
| Vendors | Vendor portal (AppFolio) or add-ons for bids (Yardi) | Vendor gets a text or email link to view the job, mark it done and upload an invoice; no login needed at first |
| Make-readies | Digital make-ready board (Entrata, AppFolio, AppWork) | Simple board: one row per vacant unit, columns for paint, clean, carpet, repairs, ready date |
| Preventive maintenance | Recurring work orders | Recurring tasks: HVAC filters, smoke detectors, pool checks, fire extinguishers, auto-created on schedule |
| Inspections | Mobile inspections with photos, damage charges | Move-in/move-out checklist with photos; charges handled in the property's main software |
| Parts and inventory | Inventory tracking, purchase orders (Yardi, RealPage) | Simple parts list per job in version 1; inventory counts later |
| Reports | Many built-in and custom reports | Three reports: open work orders by age, jobs completed per tech, cost per unit |
| Who sees what | Role permissions | Manager sees everything; tech sees only work orders; vendor sees only their jobs |
| Moving data in | Direct integrations, often partner-only | Spreadsheet import with column matching and a preview before saving |

## Left out of version 1

These stay out so version 1 is small, fast and easy to sell alongside whatever a property already uses:

- **Rent, leasing and accounting:** the main software already does this, and handling money adds legal and security risk.
- **Resident portal:** useful, but the office can enter requests at first; add it once properties ask.
- **Automatic call center and AI dispatch:** expensive to build; a clear manager screen comes first.
- **Full inventory and purchase orders:** a parts list per job covers most small properties.
- **App Store and Google Play versions:** the home-screen web app works on both phones without store fees or reviews.
- **Direct connections to Yardi, AppFolio or Entrata:** usually need partner approval; spreadsheet import comes first.

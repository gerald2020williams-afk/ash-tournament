# Doorline Launch Checklist

From a new Texas LLC to the first paying property, in order. Fees and forms are from Texas and IRS sources as of November 2026. Have a CPA and an attorney confirm the tax and legal steps before you file.

## 1. Start the new LLC (about 1 to 2 weeks)

- [ ] **Pick the final company and product name.** Search the federal trademark database and the Texas business name list, and check that the website name is free. "Doorline" was a placeholder. Free. [Trademark search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/)
- [ ] **Choose a registered agent.** Either you at a Texas address (it becomes public) or a service. $0 to $150 a year.
- [ ] **File the Certificate of Formation (Form 205)** online with the Secretary of State through SOSDirect. $300. [SOSDirect](https://direct.sos.state.tx.us/)
- [ ] **File an assumed name certificate (Form 503)** if the product name is different from the LLC name. $25.
- [ ] **Get the EIN (company tax number)** online at IRS.gov. Free. [IRS EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online)
- [ ] **Write a single-member operating agreement.** Banks often ask for it.
- [ ] **Open a business bank account.** Keep all business money separate.
- [ ] **Get the Texas sales and use tax permit.** Texas taxes software subscriptions as a data processing service, on 80% of the price. Free. [Comptroller permits](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/permit/)
- [ ] **Set up bookkeeping and meet a CPA once** about software sales tax, franchise tax and yearly filings.
- [ ] **Put May 15 on the calendar every year.** Every Texas LLC files a Public Information Report by May 15, even with no tax due. [Franchise tax](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/)

## 2. Protect yourself (2 to 4 weeks, can overlap)

- [ ] **Attorney: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Data Processing Agreement, customer contract.** Start from [attorney-outline.md](attorney-outline.md). Often $1,500 to $5,000.
- [ ] **Attorney reviews the wording inside the app:** screening rules, denial notice, late fees, deposit letter, towing notices, lease signing and training lessons.
- [ ] **Business insurance:** general liability, errors and omissions, and cyber liability. Often $500 to $2,000 a year.
- [ ] **File the trademark (optional)** once the name is final. [USPTO](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks)

## 3. Put the software online (2 to 6 weeks)

- [x] **Create a GitHub account and repository.** Doorline now lives in this repo under `doorline/`.
- [ ] **Buy the domain name.** About $15 a year.
- [ ] **Create the Supabase projects:** live (Pro plan) and test. $25 a month. [Supabase](https://supabase.com/)
- [ ] **Set up error alerts and an uptime monitor** (Sentry and an uptime checker).
- [ ] **Set up a texting and calling account, plus business texting registration (10DLC).** Registration can take a couple of weeks.
- [ ] **Stripe account,** for billing customers, plus Stripe Connect for online rent. [Stripe](https://stripe.com/)
- [ ] **Tenant screening company account.** Usually the applicant pays.
- [ ] **Connect the app to the database and run every test** on the test copy first: money, screens, accessibility, stress and security.
- [ ] **A security professional reviews the app.**
- [ ] **The CPA checks the money math** against a real month.
- [ ] **Practice restoring a backup** into the test copy.

## 4. First customer, then selling (1 to 3 months)

- [ ] **Pick a pilot property and sign a pilot agreement:** free or heavily discounted for 60 to 90 days, in exchange for honest feedback and a testimonial.
- [ ] **Import the pilot data and train the manager and techs.**
- [ ] **Run the pilot and fix what it finds.**
- [ ] **Make the website, a one-page flyer and pricing.**
- [ ] **Pitch owners with portfolios,** leading with the pilot's results.
- [ ] **Sign the first paying customer.**

## Later: close the two old LLCs

- [ ] Look up the status of old LLC #1 (SOSDirect and the Comptroller franchise tax search).
- [ ] Look up the status of old LLC #2.
- [ ] Ask the CPA the right way to close each one. If an LLC is in good standing: get tax clearance, then file a Certificate of Termination (Form 651, $40).

# Doorline vs. the Competition

Nov 8, 2026

## Bottom line

Doorline beats the big programs on the maintenance side and on ease of use, and is priced in the same range. It does not yet match them on money features: online rent payments, tenant screening, listings and full accounting are still demos or basic, and the big programs have proven themselves on millions of units for years.

This comparison uses what each company and its reviewers publish. It cannot measure the competitors' speed or reliability, because that needs logins to their systems. The fair head-to-head test is the timed task race at the end: the same 10 jobs, done by the same manager, in Doorline and in her current software.

**The pitch that holds up:** built by a maintenance supervisor, easier for new staff, stronger tools for the maintenance team, with clear pricing and local support.

## What users complain about, and Doorline's answer

| Complaint from reviews | Who it's about | Doorline's answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Support is slow or hard to reach | AppFolio (most frequent con), Entrata | A local owner who answers, plus Message support in the app with the screen attached |
| Too many steps for simple tasks, steep learning curve | AppFolio, RealPage OneSite | Show Me How walks through the real buttons; cheat sheets for new staff |
| Slow, freezes, dated screens | RealPage OneSite, Entrata | Every screen loads in under a third of a second at 1,000 units, even on a slow phone |
| Hidden or unclear pricing, yearly increases | Entrata, RealPage, Rent Manager, AppFolio | One published price, no setup fee, 60 days' notice before any change |
| Payment and accounting mistakes | AppFolio | Money in exact cents, nothing deleted, every change logged; tested math |

AppFolio is rated 4.5 of 5 across about 1,900 Capterra reviews, so most users are happy; these are the repeated complaints, not the typical experience ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/92228/AppFolio-Property-Manager/reviews/)). RealPage OneSite is rated 4.3 of 5 on 25 reviews ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/225682/ONEsite/reviews/)).

## Where the big programs still win

- **Money features:** online rent payments, tenant screening, listings and full accounting are working products for them; for Doorline they are demos until the partner companies are connected.
- **Track record:** years of real customers and huge portfolios. Doorline has a load test, not yet a paying property.
- **Integrations:** banks, accounting firms, insurance, utility billing and hundreds of partners.
- **Support teams:** large staffs and training departments, even if reviewers say they're slow.
- **Specialty housing:** affordable housing compliance, student and senior housing, commercial.

The smart move is to sell Doorline where these matter least first: small and mid-size properties that care most about maintenance and ease of use.
